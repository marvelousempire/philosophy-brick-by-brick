# Five-Star Treatment — Gateway to Heaven Entry

We do not tell somebody no. We give them options. Even when the message is "get out of here," the delivery is a menu of next moves.

Origin: the Charges family game, first era. We stayed away from negative words — no, can't, never, I don't — and used synonyms that annotated what we wanted instead of what we refused.

Graceful validation is the programming expression of this: when a build fails, the system does not stop at the failure. It offers the next option. The wall may not stand, but the path forward is always visible.
